## 2. Competitive environment

As to the introduction of new supply in other cities, it could be something we would look at, but it would have to be a situation in which we can invest in it accretively, and we would also have to have very high confidence that the destination can be competitive in what is a very competitive marketplace. I do think that the competitive superiority of Las Vegas as a convention destination is so well established that I do not know that anybody is tremendously worried about new convention supply in other American cities. What I have noticed over the past couple of years, as competition has come - whether that is through iGaming or sports betting - is watching these operators reinvigorate their offerings.
