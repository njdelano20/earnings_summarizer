What's driving each segment:

  • Client: Sequentially we expect revenue to be up approximately 13%, driven by strong double-digit growth in both our data center and embedded segments and a modest decline in our client gaming segment with slight growth in client offset by strong double-digit decline in gaming. Looking to the second half of the year, we are planning for a softer PC market as higher memory and component costs weigh on demand.
  • Embedded: With Venice and MI455X now in production, initial Helios shipments set to begin this quarter, Ryzen Pro CPUs driving continued commercial share gains and our embedded segment returning to strong year-over-year growth. We actually think the Embedded business will give us additional tailwind on the gross margin side in 2027.
  • Gaming: In gaming, revenue declined 31% year-over-year to $779 million, primarily due to lower semi-custom sales at this stage of the console cycle. Gaming graphics revenue also declined year-over-year as higher industry-wide component costs contributed to higher graphics card prices and weighed on overall demand.

5. Competitive environment

Against this backdrop, we expect our client business to perform better than the market, driven by the strength of our Ryzen portfolio and growing commercial adoption. Customer demand for Venice is stronger than for any prior EPYC generation, and we expect to continue growing market share across cloud and enterprise in the coming quarters. We gained x86 server revenue share year-over-year as customers expanded deployments of both fifth-gen EPYC Turin and fourth-gen EPYC Genoa families.

6. Product development

  • In data center AI, the growing number and scale of Helios and MI450 Series Instinct deployments position the business for significant growth in the second half of the year with growth accelerating in 2027.
  • To build on this momentum, Dell, HP, Lenovo, Asus and others launched a broad portfolio of new commercial PCs powered by our latest generation Ryzen AI Pro 400 series processors.
  • Looking beyond Helios, we plan to launch a new rack-scale AI platform every year with each generation delivering significant performance, efficiency and TCO gains.
  • At our Advanced AI event, we launched Helios, our rack-scale AI platform combining EPYC Venice CPUs, MI450-Series GPUs, Pensando networking and ROCm software.
  • Anthropic will deploy up to 2 gigawatts of MI450 series GPUs in Helios with deployment of the first gigawatt beginning in the first half of 2027.
  • Across a broad range of inferencing workloads, Helios delivers up to 15% more throughput at the same rack power and up to 30% more tokens per dollar than the competition.
